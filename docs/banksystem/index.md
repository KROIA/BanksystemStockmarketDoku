# BankSystem

BankSystem gives every player a bank account for money and items. You put items in at a Bank Terminal, take out cash at an ATM, share accounts with friends, automate your farms with redstone, and even found a company with its own shares.

BankSystem is also the wallet for [StockMarket](../stockmarket/index.md). New to both mods? Start with [Getting started](../getting-started.md).

## Good to know

- **Not every item fits in the bank.** Your server admin decides which items are allowed. Damaged or enchanted items can't be stored.
- **Money in the bank is safe.** Cash in your inventory, a chest or a pile is just items that other players can take.
- **One account, many servers.** On a network of several servers, your account is the same on all of them.
- **Admins can freeze accounts.** A frozen account keeps everything in it, but nothing moves until an admin unfreezes it.
