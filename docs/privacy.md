# Privacy

This site has no accounts, no ads and no cookies. This page lists all data that leaves your browser when you use the site, who receives it and why.

*Last updated: 25 November 2026*

## Who is responsible

Questions about your data, or a request to see or delete it: write to [alexkrieg@gmx.ch](mailto:alexkrieg@gmx.ch).

## Hosting: GitHub Pages

GitHub, Inc. (USA) hosts the site on GitHub Pages. When you open a page, GitHub logs your IP address to keep the service secure. This happens whether or not you have a GitHub account. We can't see these logs.

- **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in delivering the site safely.
- **More:** [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

All fonts, pictures and scripts of the site come from GitHub Pages too. The site doesn't load fonts from Google or any other font service.

## Visitor statistics: GoatCounter

We count visits with GoatCounter. It shows us which pages people read and which ones need work.

- GoatCounter sets no cookies.
- It stores only totals per page and per hour or day, for example "12 views on this page today" or "20 visitors used Firefox". Totals can't be traced back to a person.
- What goes into the totals: the page address, the page you came from, browser and version, operating system, country, language and screen width.
- GoatCounter works out the country from your IP address. To tell new visitors from returning ones, it keeps your IP address and browser details in memory for up to 8 hours. It never writes them to its database.
- Words you type into the search box are counted the same way, as a total per search term. They show us which topics are missing.

**Turn it off:** open any page with `#toggle-goatcounter` at the end of the address. A message confirms that this browser is no longer counted. Your browser stores this choice. Open the same address again to undo it.

- **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in improving the documentation.
- **More:** [GoatCounter privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy)

## Feedback form: Tally

The **?** next to a heading and the **Tell us** button at the end of a page open a feedback form from Tally (Tally BV, Ghent, Belgium). The form loads only when you click. Before that, nothing is sent to Tally.

- The form sends what you type, the address of the page and the heading you clicked.
- The contact field is optional. Leave it empty if you don't want a reply.
- Tally stores the answers on servers in the EU. Only we can read them. We use them only to improve this documentation and delete them when we no longer need them.
- When the form opens, Tally saves a draft of your answers and a random respondent ID in your browser's storage. Tally sets no cookies.

To have your feedback deleted, contact us (see [Who is responsible](#who-is-responsible)).

- **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, your consent: you choose to send the form.
- **More:** [Tally privacy policy](https://tally.so/help/privacy-policy)

## Stored in your browser

The site remembers whether you chose light or dark mode, in your browser's local storage. This choice never leaves your device.

## Your rights

You have the right to see, correct and delete your data, to limit how it is used, to object to its use and to take it with you (Art. 15–21 GDPR). You can withdraw your consent at any time. To use any of these rights, contact us (see [Who is responsible](#who-is-responsible)).

The visitor statistics can't be linked to a person, so we can't find your data in them.

You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the one in the country where you live.
