# Trading company shares

Players can found companies in BankSystem and print shares of them. When a company opens a market for its shares, you buy and sell them on the stock market like any other item. This page shows where to find company markets and what owning a share gives you.

## Finding company markets

1. Open the trading screen at a **Stock Market Block**.
2. Click **Select Market** in the top bar. In **Item / Item** mode, click **Select...** next to **I HAVE:** or **I WANT:**.
3. Open the **Companies** tab. Normal items are on the **Commodities** tab.

Each company share has its own card picture, so you can tell the companies apart. **Sort: Supply** puts the companies first that have printed the largest part of their shares.

## Buying and selling shares

Shares trade exactly like other items. Read [Trading basics](trading.md) for market and limit orders.

- **Bought shares** land in your bank account as **Company Share** items.
- **To sell shares** you hold as items, put them into the bank at a [Bank Terminal](../banksystem/bank-terminal.md) first.
- The **supply** of a company is the number of shares it has printed, compared with the most it may print.

## Dividends

A company can pay a **dividend**: an amount of money, or of an item, for each share. Every holder gets *shares × amount per share*.

!!! note "Keep your shares in the bank"
    Only shares in a bank account count for dividends, including shares that are locked in an open sell order. Shares in your inventory or in a chest get nothing.

The dividend goes into the bank account that holds the shares. See [Payouts and dividends](../banksystem/payouts.md) for how a company pays them.

## Paused and closed company markets

The company's managers can pause its market. The founder can close it for good.

- **Paused:** the trading screen shows a red banner, usually **Under Maintenance**, and you can't trade. Your open orders on this market are cancelled and refunded right away. When the company resumes trading, the market starts with an empty order book.
- **Paused because of duplicated shares:** the banner says *Closed: share duplication detected (more shares banked than stamped)*. More shares turned up in the banks than the company ever printed. A company admin must check it and reopen the market.
- **Closed for good:** the market is gone, with its price history. Your shares stay in your bank account and in your inventory. You can still hold them and trade them in person.
